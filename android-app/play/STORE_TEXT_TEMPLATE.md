## App Name
Chess AI

## Short Description (<=80)
Play chess against AI models with timers, PvP and AI-vs-AI modes.

## Full Description
Chess AI is a modern chess app where you can:

- play against another person on one device
- play against AI with configurable model/provider
- run AI vs AI matches and compare model strength
- choose time controls (bullet, blitz, rapid, classic)
- export PGN and keep local game history
- track local Elo leaderboard for AI models

The app is optimized for mobile play and includes an interactive move list, board flip, and quick game controls.

You can connect your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint and test different models in real chess games.
